A Neighborhood Staple

Tucked behind the B68 bus stop on Coney Island Avenue, Neptune Pizza has been slinging hand-tossed pies to Brighton Beach for generations. It's a small, no-frills spot — a couple of tables, a hot oven, and a family that's been doing this for decades.

Thin, crisp crust. A fair ratio of sauce to cheese. Halal options on request. Whether you're grabbing a quick slice after the beach or ordering a full pie for the family, Neptune keeps it simple and keeps it fresh.
