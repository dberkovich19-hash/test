Supply — Permian & US Exports

  • Permian natural gas (NGL-rich associated gas) tracking ~29.5 Bcf/d in 2026, up 1.7% YoY. Crude production mild ~1% decline to 6.52 MMbbl/d.
  • NGL takeaway capacity expanding from ~4.4 MMb/d → ~5.5 MMb/d by 2030 via Bahia NGL Pipeline (ExxonMobil/Enterprise, 600 Mb/d), Targa Speedway, and Lone Star Express II.
  • US LPG exports averaged ~1.735 MMb/d in Apr–Sep 2025, roughly flat YoY. Material new export capacity (Enterprise HCT +300,000 b/d) not until H2 2026 — near-term upside limited.
  • China's retreat from US propane (~48% YoY decline) is the dominant demand headwind for FOB Mont Belvieu prices. OPIS: "above-average terminal fees, wonky arb, and volatile freight."

Demand — Asia & Key Importers

  • China PDH: 3 new plants (SP Chemical 800kt/y, Sinopec Zhenhai 600kt/y, Yuanjin 750kt/y) scheduled 2026 — but SP Chemical and Yuanjin startups likely delayed on poor PDH margins.   • Japan/Korea: Japan ~$578/MT, Korea ~$547/MT Q1 2026. Korea US imports down ~20% YoY in 2025. Both capitalizing on lower spot prices to secure US cargoes.

  • BLPG3 active and elevated: ~$52,800/day circulating in market (social media sourced, unconfirmed vs Clarksons/Fearnleys). Pre-crisis 2026 forecast was $40,000/day avg.

OPEC+ & Geopolitics

  • Hormuz effectively closed to US/Israeli/Western-allied shipping since 28 Feb. Iran granted selective exemptions to China, Russia, India, Iraq, Pakistan flagged vessels (5 March).
